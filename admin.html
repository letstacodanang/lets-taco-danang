STAFF GUIDE
Everything you need to run the restaurant. Read before your first shift.
1
NEW ORDER ARRIVES
→Red badge on Home tab shows new orders
→Order appears at top with PLACED status
→Check name, items, address, contact method
2
ACCEPT AND NOTIFY
→Tap Accept Order — message window opens automatically
→Tap SEND MESSAGE to notify customer via WhatsApp or Zalo
⚠Delivery orders must be paid BEFORE cooking starts
3
COOK THE ORDER
→Tap Prepping when preparing ingredients
→Tap Cooking when tacos are on the grill
→Each tap auto-notifies customer of their order status
4
DISPATCH DELIVERY
→Tap Assign Driver, enter driver name and phone
→Customer auto-receives driver name and contact number
5
ORDER COMPLETE
→Tap Delivered when customer confirms receipt
→Thank you message sent. Loyalty points added automatically.
PICKUP ORDERS
→Pickup customers pay when they arrive, no prepayment needed
→After cooking tap Delivered when they collect their order
IMPORTANT RULES
1.Never cancel an order without calling the customer first
2.Delivery orders must be paid before cooking starts
3.Always send the notification message at each status change
4.Problems? WhatsApp owner: 0909 923 941
5.Never share the admin password with anyone
LOYALTY POINTS
→Every taco ordered = 1 point added automatically
→10 points = 15% discount on next order applied automatically
→See each customer points in the Customers tab
