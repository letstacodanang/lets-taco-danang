The Story

Some chefs cook food.
He cooks feeling.

For over 20 years, he traveled the world cooking in professional kitchens, eating on street corners, tasting everything every country had to offer. One thing became undeniably clear:

"No food on earth hits the soul like a real taco. The kind that stops you mid-bite. The kind that makes your eyes close."

Everywhere he went, people were trying to make tacos. But nobody could quite get it right. The tortilla was wrong. The birria wasn't slow-cooked long enough. The magic was missing.

So he made a decision. He started small — a single stand, a griddle, and 20 years of skill. Within months, word spread. Within just 9 months, Let's Taco became Da Nang's highest-rated Mexican restaurant — loved by locals, expats and food lovers from over 30 countries.

This is just the beginning. His goal: real Mexican tacos, for every soul, in every city, on every continent. Because everyone deserves to feel at home — even 10,000 miles from Mexico.
